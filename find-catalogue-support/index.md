# Find Catalogue – Support

If you need help using Find Catalogue, please contact us using the details below.

## Contact Support

**Email:** bmbapps@icloud.com  
We aim to respond within 48 hours.

## Common Help Topics

### • Problems adding or editing find records
Make sure you are using the latest version of the app. Try restarting the app or your device.

### • Unable to add photographs
Ensure you have granted the app permission to access your camera or Photos library.

### • Location or map problems
Check that Location Services are enabled for Find Catalogue. Interactive maps also require an internet connection.

### • Issues exporting or sharing records
Check that the receiving app is available on your device. Try exporting again after closing and reopening Find Catalogue.

## Additional Information
- Find Catalogue stores your records locally on your device.  
- You can export records using the built-in sharing tools.  
- No account is required to use the app.

If you continue to have issues, please email us and we will be happy to assist.
